## Why Powderhorn Park Residents Need a Water Softener

Powderhorn Park homes drink the City of Minneapolis supply. The City of Minneapolis 2025 record lists average total hardness of 94 mg/L (about 5.5 gpg), within a range of 80 to 113 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates moderately hard. The City gives no separate Powderhorn Park figure, so a test at your tap gives the number for your house.

A Macalester College planning report says most Powderhorn housing was built by about 1920, and the mix runs from single-family homes to duplexes and three-story brick apartment buildings, with many single-family homes later divided into smaller units. A building with several units can share one water line, so the technician confirms which line a softener serves. At 5.5 gpg, a household of four using about 300 gallons a day removes about 1,650 grains daily, so a 32,000-grain salt-based softener regenerates about every 19 days.

## About Powderhorn Park Water Quality

The City of Minneapolis draws Mississippi River water, treats it at the Fridley and Columbia Heights plants and softens it with lime before distribution. Plant readings in 2025 ran from 4.7 to 6.6 gpg across the year. Buildings from the early 1900s can have older plumbing, and a tap test shows what reaches your home.
