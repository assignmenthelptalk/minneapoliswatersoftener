### Lead and Arsenic Removal

Lead usually enters water from service lines and home plumbing, not from the utility's source. A certified filter or [reverse osmosis system](/reverse-osmosis-installation/) reduces lead and arsenic at the tap. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: Minneapolis lead service line inventory and replacement program.]</mark> Check your service line material as well as filtering.

### Chloramine and Chlorine Removal

Chlorine and chloramine are disinfectants, and the City treats its supply at the Fridley and Columbia Heights plants. A catalytic carbon filter reduces both, along with the taste and smell they leave. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: whether the City uses chlorine or chloramine, from its water quality report.]</mark>

### PFAS Water Filter

PFAS are a group of persistent chemicals, and a carbon block or reverse osmosis filter certified for PFAS reduces them at the tap. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: the City of Minneapolis PFAS results from its current report.]</mark>

### Nitrate Filter Installation

Nitrate is a concern mainly for [private wells](/well-water-softener-minneapolis-mn/) and for infants, and an ion exchange or reverse osmosis system reduces it. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: nitrate result from the City's report against the 10 ppm limit.]</mark>

### VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) Filter

A carbon filter reduces volatile organic compounds, which come from solvents and fuels. A water test shows whether any are present before a filter is chosen.

> **Not a good fit if...** your test shows no contaminant of concern. A filter adds cost without benefit, and the technician tells you so.
