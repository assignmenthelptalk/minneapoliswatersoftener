## Why Linden Hills Residents Need a Water Softener

Linden Hills homes drink the same City supply as the rest of Minneapolis. The City of Minneapolis 2025 record lists average total hardness of 94 mg/L (about 5.5 gpg), within a range of 80 to 113 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates moderately hard. The City publishes no separate Linden Hills figure, so a test at your tap gives the number for your house.

The neighborhood was first developed in the 1880s, and the Como-Harriet streetcar line ran through it by 1891, so many homes carry decades of plumbing. HUD's neighborhood profile lists a mix of ramblers, colonials, Tudors and bungalows. At 5.5 gpg, a household of four using about 300 gallons a day removes about 1,650 grains daily, so a 32,000-grain salt-based softener regenerates about every 19 days.
