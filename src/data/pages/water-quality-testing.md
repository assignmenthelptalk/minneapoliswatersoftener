## What Does Water Quality Testing Measure in Minneapolis, MN?

Seven tests cover hardness, total dissolved solids, iron, pH, chlorine, sulfur and heavy metals.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">


![Free Hard Water Test Minneapolis MN](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Free Hard Water Test

A [hard water](/water-softener-guides/hard-water-minneapolis-mn/) test reads calcium and magnesium in grains per gallon. The City lists 4.7 to 6.6 gpg at its plants, and the test shows your home's number so a softener is sized correctly.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![TDS Testing Minneapolis MN](/placeholders/card.svg)

### TDS Testing

Total dissolved solids measures all dissolved minerals in milligrams per liter. A high reading points to [reverse osmosis](/reverse-osmosis-installation/) or another treatment for drinking water.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Iron Content Testing Minneapolis MN](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Iron Content Testing

Iron stains fixtures and laundry and fouls softener resin. A reading above the softener's limit means an iron filter should go in first.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![pH Level Testing Minneapolis MN](/placeholders/card.svg)

### pH Level Testing

pH shows whether water is acidic or alkaline. Low pH corrodes pipes, and the reading guides material and treatment choices.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Chlorine Testing Minneapolis MN](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Chlorine Testing

Chlorine is the disinfectant in city water, and the City of Minneapolis treats its supply at its plants. A tap test shows the level at your home, which affects taste and odor.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Sulfur Testing Minneapolis MN](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Sulfur Testing

Sulfur gives a rotten-egg smell, and it is mainly a [private well](/well-water-softener-minneapolis-mn/) issue. A positive test points to a hydrogen sulfide filter.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Heavy Metals Testing Minneapolis MN](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Heavy Metals Testing

A metals panel checks for lead, arsenic and similar [contaminants](/water-contaminant-removal-minneapolis-mn/). A home test shows what reaches your tap through your own plumbing. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: Minneapolis lead service line program.]</mark>

## City vs. Well Water Testing in Minneapolis

City water arrives with a published report, and well water has no utility test, so the owner has to test it. The City's report covers its own supply, and a private well needs its own panel at least yearly <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: cite recommended frequency]</mark>.
