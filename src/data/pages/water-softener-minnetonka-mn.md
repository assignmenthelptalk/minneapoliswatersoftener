## Why Minnetonka Residents Need a Water Softener

Minnetonka uses groundwater, not Mississippi River water. The City of Minnetonka's 2022 and 2023 drinking water reports say its 18 wells draw from the Prairie du Chien-Jordan and Jordan aquifers. Groundwater usually carries more dissolved calcium and magnesium than softened river water, but the hardness figure is not confirmed here. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: hardness from the current Minnetonka drinking water report.]</mark>

For comparison, the City of Minneapolis 2025 record lists average total hardness of 94 mg/L (about 5.5 gpg), within a range of 80 to 113 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates moderately hard.
